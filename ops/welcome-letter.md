## What you will work on

Foundry work takes three forms.

**Funded engagements.** A client pays a fee, retains the intellectual property, and expects professional delivery. These carry the tightest obligations.

**Claude certification.** Everyone works toward the Claude Certified Developer credential, which is what qualifies the Foundry for the Claude Partner Network. The preparation is coursework and the Foundry reimburses the exam fee (repeat attempts are a personal expense).

**AI Foundry projects.** Everything the Foundry builds on its own account: internal tools, thought leadership and open repositories, and unfunded engagements for outside organizations such as nonprofits, university units, and early-stage ventures. The standard of work is identical to a funded engagement. Where an outside organization is involved, its confidentiality and intellectual property terms apply in full.

## Time expectation

This is a 3.0 credit hour course and the expectation is **10 hours per week**.

Work beyond 10 hours a week is expected when staffed to a paid client engagement.

## Remuneration

Conditional on collection of paying client fees, students will be reimbursed via their BYU student financial account for AI tooling usage up to agreed project limits. The timing of this reimbursement depends on client fee collection and will be done promptly after fee collection. If there is sufficient AI Foundry revenue, students may also receive an additional stipend posted to their student accounts.

## Confidentiality, intellectual property, and publicity

Ownership is set by the agreement governing each piece of work, and it is not the same for every kind of Foundry project.

- **On a funded client engagement, deliverables and intellectual property belong to the client.** They do not belong to me and they do not belong to BYU.
- **On an AI Foundry project, ownership depends on what the project is.** A Foundry tool or Foundry repository belongs to the Foundry. An unfunded outside engagement is governed by that engagement's agreement. 
- At the request of a client or partner, I will sign **Nondisclosure Agreements** covering the work I do on that engagement.
- **Check individual agreements for what can be shared publicly.** In many cases, the details of our work may need to remain confidential by client request.
